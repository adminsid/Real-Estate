# ADR 006: Person, Evidence, Documents, Events, and Consent

Prime America models one Person with several roles rather than duplicating a contact for each business context. Listings and Transactions belong to a Property; documents are classified by Property, Listing, or Transaction scope regardless of whether the form came from an MLS, the office, or the state, and signed versions are retained as authoritative evidence. Conflicts resolve in this order: signed document, owner- or agent-confirmed fact, then public observation. Transactions are created only from a defined Business Event such as an executed Listing agreement or accepted offer—not from a draft or page load. Brand Hub and Director remain specialized tools that share Listing, Marketing Audience, and Asset IDs. Marketing permission is recorded independently for phone, email, and text, including capture and withdrawal history; Person data is not sold.
