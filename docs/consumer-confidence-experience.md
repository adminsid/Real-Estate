# Consumer confidence and the purpose of saving

Date: November 15, 2026. Status: product requirements informed by official-source research; the first consumer-interface implementation is recorded in [deployment verification](verification/consumer-confidence-preview-2026-11-15.md). The user endorsed the preceding search-start, card, photo, accessibility and navigation recommendations and added confidence, rights and professional accountability as core requirements. This document does not claim that the new experience or legal workflows are implemented, approved legal forms, or a completed compliance review.

Related: [language and trust guide](search-trust-and-language-guide.md), [NY requirements research](research/ny-consumer-confidence-requirements-2026-11-15.md), [portal litigation research](research/portal-trust-and-litigation-lessons-2026-11-15.md), [website vocabulary](website-vocabulary-guide.md).

## Product purpose

Help a person understand a property, organize their decision, identify the people involved, exercise their choices and take the next step with accurate expectations. Belonging should come from continuity, control, useful assistance and respectful treatment.

The heart begins a private shortlist. It must lead to something useful beyond retaining a listing ID: reasons for interest, questions, a comparison, a viewing plan and an understandable record of what happened. Saving does not by itself hire a broker, request contact, authorize marketing or establish financial readiness. Actual representation and duties must reflect the real arrangement and conduct, including relationships formed outside this platform.

## Your search

Use a human destination name such as **Your search**, with **Saved properties** as a clear subsection. Permit browsing and a basic shortlist without mandatory registration. Explain current storage accurately: browser-only saves can be lost and are visible to people using that browser. Offer optional consumer identity and cross-device continuity when that service exists; do not enroll consumers into the private brokerage Workspace.

Proposed structure:

| Area | Consumer value | Boundaries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Saved properties | Remember why each place matters; compare source facts and known recurring costs | Preserve MLS permissions/removals; explain unavailable items without retaining prohibited listing data |
| My questions | Separate known facts, missing information and questions to ask | Private by default; missing information is not a defect, a zero cost or a refusal by the seller |
| People | Identify listing contact, one's chosen representative, brokerage and optional transaction professionals | Show verified or explicitly self-reported roles; no silent reassignment or assumption that an unknown buyer lacks an outside agent |
| Viewings | See drafts, requests, proposed alternatives and confirmations | A chosen time is a preference until the responsible professional confirms it |
| Documents and decisions | Find documents received, their meaning, versions and relevant next action | An upload/receipt/acknowledgment is not proof of informed understanding, execution, review or compliance |
| Next step | One relevant action with help available | Self-selected stages; no arbitrary percentage, readiness score or pressure to sign |

Keep personal search, notes and transaction records out of the public URL and share preview. Sharing a listing link does not share private notes, contact details, documents or messages. A consumer separately chooses what to share and with whom; access can be scoped and revoked subject to lawful retention.

Avoid medical, financial, voucher and identity-document collection in ordinary search/local storage. Secure accounts, scoped access, file controls and a retention schedule are prerequisites to sensitive document storage.

## Put understanding beside an action

Keep the start page and cards simple. Offer concise help at the point of a decision, alongside access to the official document and a responsible professional. Do not force everyone through a legal tutorial before searching.

Each explanation answers: **What is this? Why am I seeing it? What does this action do? What can I ask or do next?** Use visible labels and accessible official translations where available. Summaries supplement prescribed documents; they do not silently change their wording or stand in for required delivery.

| Decision point | Information to provide | Operational requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Searching | Permitted facts, costs and unknowns; concise rights/SOP links | Required licensed identity, attribution and conspicuous fair-housing notice survive branding, embedding and shared pages |
| Asking about a property | Named recipient, company, actual role and what will be shared | The label must match the route; provide the relevant notice/disclosure at its actual trigger |
| Preparing a viewing | Existing-agent / no-agent / not-sure choices; role explanation; agreed requirements | Choices aid routing and explanation; they are not consent to representation or dual agency |
| Considering representation | Services, duration, property/geographic scope, compensation and any exclusivity | Separate agreement and informed choice; identify whether a requirement is law, applicable participation rule or disclosed brokerage policy |
| Reviewing a property | Seller disclosures, inspections, reports and unresolved questions | Correct property type, jurisdiction, document version and exemptions; identify seller statements versus independent professional findings |
| Offer and contract | Responsible agent/attorney contacts, documents received and questions outstanding | Route individualized legal advice to the appropriate professional; observe actual pre-contract requirements |
| Financing and closing | Chosen professionals, actual estimates and verified instructions | Disclose relevant business relationships; no automatic lender selection or unsupported financial/legal readiness badge |

## New York requirements that affect the design

Article 12-A governs licensing and agency matters, with implementing regulations; seller property-condition disclosures are in Article 14. Federal, state and city fair-housing and rental rules are additional layers. Rules need jurisdiction and transaction scope; NY implementation cannot automatically be carried into national/international expansion.

- **Agency:** RPL443 has defined residential scope and role-specific timing. It explicitly distinguishes the disclosure form from a contract. Never present an inquiry acknowledgment as an agreement to hire, or infer informed dual-agency consent from a bundled checkbox. Refusal to acknowledge requires the legally appropriate licensee record/workflow, not a fabricated consumer signature. [Current RPL443](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/443).
- **Buyer agreements:** NAR's applicable participant touring rule is distinct from a statewide statute. NY S9564/A8910 were still pending when checked. Which professional is working for which party, the actual participation rules, published SOP and agreement terms govern the applicable workflow. Do not require every visitor to hire a buyer's agent to view a listing. [NAR consumer guide](https://www.nar.realtor/the-facts/consumer-guide-to-written-buyer-agreements), [S9564](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S9564).
- **Fair housing:** Notice posting and first-substantive-contact delivery are separate obligations. Record the appropriate delivery/acknowledgment/refusal evidence and retention; a homepage link alone does not prove individual delivery. Apply consistent inquiry and showing procedures and offer an appropriate private accommodation channel. Do not segregate voucher holders or use protected traits/proxies to recommend where someone belongs. [DOS guidance](https://dos.ny.gov/fair-housing-guidance), [NYC guidance](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/cchr/downloads/pdf/materials/FairHouse_FAQs-Landlord-English.pdf).
- **Property condition:** Applicable sellers supply the prescribed statement before the buyer signs a binding sale contract. Condos/co-ops and statutory exemptions require separate handling. The form does not replace inspections or guarantee condition. The prior $500 substitute-credit option was removed in 2024; use the current effective form rather than an outdated generic URL. [RPL462](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/RPP/462), [current DOS form](https://dos.ny.gov/property-condition-disclosure-statement-eff-7125).
- **NYC rentals:** FARE fee and advertising duties require identifying the actual parties, payer and charges. A tenant cannot be compelled to hire an agent to see/rent identifiable inventory. Never build a universal representation gate around rental tours. [DCWP](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/about/FAQ-Broker-Fees.page).
- **Rental IDX:** A dated OneKey FAQ describes passive-IDX implications and affected rental removal. Verify current written entitlement/rules before making claims about NYC rental coverage. Direct contact routing does not erase the publisher's own obligations. A fee-rule presumption is not automatic creation of every possible agency relationship. [OneKey FAQ](https://support.onekeymls.com/hc/en-us/articles/38994162809236-Fairness-in-Apartment-Rental-Expenses-FARE-Act-Frequently-Asked-Questions).

These are selected requirements, with detailed scope and unresolved questions in the linked research; they are not an exhaustive law register. Do not mark a rule inapplicable solely because an unreliable feed field appears to establish an exemption.

## Contact and viewing experience

Display the actual licensed recipient and company. State the representation role when verified; otherwise identify it as a question to resolve. Distinguish the company providing the listing, the platform operator and the consumer's own representative.

Candidate contextual copy, subject to actual implementation and brokerage approval:

> This request goes to [name/team] at [brokerage].
>
> Their role: [verified role, or a clear request to confirm the role].
>
> Preferred viewing: [date and time, time zone]. The appointment will be confirmed separately.
>
> Do you already work with an agent? [Yes / No / I'm not sure]

Show whom the consumer will contact before collecting a message. If Prime America receives and forwards it, disclose that route. If an email application is opened, say so. A successful provider delivery is not proof that a human has read or accepted the request.

Use evidence-backed states:

`Draft → Received by platform → Sent/Delivered to named recipient → Reply received → Showing confirmed / alternative proposed / declined / canceled`

Display only states supported by the communication channel. Provide recovery and escalation for failure/unanswered requests. Share an existing representative's contact or invite that professional into a thread only with appropriate authorization. Ordinary messages, required document acknowledgments, dual-agency consent, representation contracts and optional marketing preferences remain separate decisions.

## Professional accountability

The professional side needs an assigned responsible person and supervising brokerage, applicable requirements, outstanding actions, versioned records and escalation. Prime America supervises its own associated licensees; it must not claim supervisory control over an external listing company's agents.

For each relevant event retain the appropriate person/role, transaction, rule/version, document/version, delivery channel/result, acknowledgment or signature actually collected, timestamp and correction history. Use rule-specific retention and restricted access. Sending, opening, acknowledging receipt, signing and professional review are distinct states. Handle declined acknowledgments, scope uncertainty and exemptions under an actual reviewed procedure; no silent bypass and no universal denial of service.

The operating rule register needs authority category (law/contract/company standard), source, jurisdiction, property/transaction scope, trigger, effective dates, exceptions, owner, approved content/form version, last verification and material-change review triggers. The Principal Broker and appropriate NY legal reviewer resolve scope and approve the operating process; software records and supports that work.

## Explain industry concerns through our conduct

Provide a concise **How this platform works** page about actual inquiry recipients, representation, paid placements/referral economics, lender choice, data sharing and correction/complaint channels. Publish only commitments the running system and business arrangements support.

An optional sourced learning article can discuss industry cases, with dated statuses and clear distinctions between allegations, pleadings rulings, settlements and proven liability. For example, the FTC announced a Zillow/Redfin rental-advertising resolution in August 2026; that is different from an adjudication of deceptive contact-agent routing. Other proceedings have separate issues and stages. [FTC resolution](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/08/ftc-secures-order-resolving-antitrust-concerns-zillow-redfin-agreement), [case research](research/portal-trust-and-litigation-lessons-2026-11-15.md).

Good faith is demonstrated by accurate routing, informed choice, lawful fees, consistent treatment, meaningful supervision and prompt correction. Statements such as “fully compliant,” “lawsuit-proof,” or “safer than a competitor” do not establish those outcomes. A legal-information page cannot substitute for the actual operating behavior.

## Delivery sequence and acceptance

1. Apply the endorsed simple start page, card, photo and bottom-navigation direction. Keep legal identity, source attribution and appropriate notices visible.
2. Make saved properties useful through **Your search**, comparisons, private reasons/questions and clear storage/sharing explanations. Do not collect sensitive transaction files in local storage.
3. Build accurate recipient/role presentation and a reliable communication backend before claiming sent, delivered or confirmed platform-managed showings. Add contextual rights/term explanations and applicable notice/form delivery.
4. Add professional task ownership, versioned records, scoped access and broker review; then extend to offers, due diligence and transaction coordination.

Test with first-time shoppers and older adults without coaching. They should be able to find a saved property, name the message recipient, explain that an agency disclosure differs from hiring, distinguish a request from a confirmed showing, find applicable rights/help and control sharing. Measure these outcomes, response reliability, unresolved questions and disclosure-process performance alongside actual qualified inquiries.

## Current implementation gap

At the research checkpoint, the search held browser-local saved IDs and opened email drafts. The subsequent first interface release adds the search start page, useful browser-owned planning, contextual explanations and transparency pages. Contact still opens an email draft. Consumer accounts/cross-device storage, provider-backed message delivery, confirmed appointments, prescribed document acknowledgment/signature, transaction records and a broker compliance dashboard remain separate implementation work. The interface does not claim these exist or that a legal requirement has been completed. Existing release and spending controls remain unchanged.
